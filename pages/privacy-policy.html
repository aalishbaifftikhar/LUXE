PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters to us.

Last reviewed: October 2026

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the LUXE website.

LUXE is currently a frontend demonstration project. It does not currently operate its own backend database, customer account system, or payment processing infrastructure. Wishlist and shopping bag features use browser local storage, while the Contact Us form uses EmailJS to deliver submitted messages.

This policy describes the website's current features and should be updated if its data practices change.

02

Information You Provide

The Contact Us page allows visitors to enter their name, email address, subject, and message.

When the contact form is submitted, this information is sent through EmailJS for email delivery. A test submission has successfully reached the configured LUXE Gmail inbox, although successful delivery is not guaranteed for every submission.

LUXE does not currently operate its own backend database to store contact form submissions. However, EmailJS processes the information needed to provide its service. Please refer to EmailJS's privacy policy for details about its data handling practices.

Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in your message.

03

How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Your message is transmitted through EmailJS to facilitate email delivery. How the third-party service processes or retains information is governed by its own policies.

LUXE does not currently operate an account, order-management, or customer database on a backend server.

04

Cookies and Local Storage

The wishlist and shopping bag features use your browser's local storage to remember your selections.

Wishlist: The luxe_wishlist key stores saved wishlist item identifiers.

Shopping Bag: The luxe_bag key stores shopping bag item information, such as product, colour, price, image reference, and quantity.

This information is stored in your browser on your device and is not automatically included in ordinary HTTP requests. It may remain until you remove it, clear your browser storage, or use a different browser or device. Anyone with appropriate access to your browser or device may potentially access locally stored data.

Google Fonts and EmailJS are external services used by this website. Their requests and data handling are subject to their respective privacy policies.

05

Third-Party Services

Google Fonts: The website loads Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to retrieve these fonts. Google may receive technical information, such as your IP address, as part of these requests. See the Google Privacy Policy.

EmailJS: The Contact Us form uses EmailJS to transmit submitted messages for email delivery. Information entered into the form is sent to this service. See the EmailJS Privacy Policy.

LUXE does not currently integrate its own payment processor, customer database, or advertising analytics platform. Third-party services may process technical information according to their own practices.

06

Data Security

LUXE is currently a frontend demonstration and does not operate its own backend database, authentication infrastructure, or payment processing system.

Contact form information is transmitted through EmailJS. The handling and protection of that information by EmailJS are subject to its service and privacy policies.

Wishlist and shopping bag information is stored in your browser's local storage. Its protection depends partly on the security of your device and browser.

This website should not be used to submit sensitive personal, financial, or account credentials through its demonstration features.

07

Your Privacy Choices

You can remove saved wishlist items and shopping bag items through the website's available controls.

You can also clear this website's local storage through your browser's site-data or developer settings. Clearing local storage may remove your saved wishlist and shopping bag contents.

If you submit a contact message, the information is sent through EmailJS. For questions about information handled by that service, consult the EmailJS Privacy Policy.

This demonstration does not currently provide an account dashboard, privacy dashboard, or automated data export tool.

08

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy describes the current LUXE website features and data practices known to us.

If the website introduces backend storage, customer accounts, payment processing, or additional third-party services, this policy should be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual changes.

Please revisit this page periodically to review the latest version.

09

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact LUXE through the Contact Us page.

LUXE's formal business registration and regulatory details have not been provided in this policy. Appropriate business information should be added if the website becomes an operating commercial store.
