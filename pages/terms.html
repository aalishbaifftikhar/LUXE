Wishlist and Browser Storage

The LUXE frontend includes an interactive wishlist that allows visitors to save and remove favourite products using the heart icon on product cards. The shopping bag feature allows visitors to review their selected products and quantities.

These features use your browser's native localStorage API to remember selections across page reloads. The wishlist uses the luxe_wishlist key, while the shopping bag uses the luxe_bag key.

Browser-based storage: Wishlist and shopping bag information is stored in your browser on your device. These features do not, by themselves, create a customer account or place an order. Information stored in the browser may remain available when you revisit the website using the same browser and device.

Persistence and limitations: Saved information may no longer be available if you clear the website's local storage, use private or incognito browsing, switch browsers, or use another device. This information is not automatically synchronized across devices, and cleared data may not be recoverable.

Scope: The luxe_wishlist key stores wishlist information, and the luxe_bag key stores shopping bag information, such as selected products and quantities. These browser features are not a customer account, checkout, or order-processing system. The Contact Us form operates separately and uses EmailJS to send submitted messages for email delivery.
